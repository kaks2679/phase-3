## 📊 Key Insights at a Glance

### Primary Finding
🌊 **Water Quantity** is the strongest predictor  
→ 96.9% of "dry" wells are non-functional

### Target Distribution
✅ Functional: 54.3% (32,259 wells)  
❌ Non-functional: 38.4% (22,824 wells)  
⚠️ Needs Repair: 7.3% (4,317 wells)

### Top Predictive Features
1. Water Quantity
2. Waterpoint Type
3. Geographic Location (longitude/latitude/region)
4. Construction Year
5. Management Type
6. Payment System

### High-Risk Regions
📍 Iringa (5,294 wells)  
📍 Shinyanga (4,982 wells)  
📍 Mbeya (4,639 wells)  
📍 Kilimanjaro (4,379 wells)  
📍 Morogoro (4,006 wells)
